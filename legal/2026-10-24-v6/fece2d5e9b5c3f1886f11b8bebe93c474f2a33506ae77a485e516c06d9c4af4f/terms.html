Operator and eligibility

Rummit is operated by Godwin Laureto, Laureto's Hilltop House, Buda, Marilog District, Davao City, Davao del Sur, Philippines. Contact support@rummit.app.

The initial marketplace is for people aged 18 or older who can enter the transaction. Accepting these terms includes your declaration that you meet that rule; it is not verified age assurance. Use an email you control, accurate information and secure sign-in. Do not impersonate anyone, evade restrictions, manipulate bids or transact with your own account. Apple or Google sign-in is optional where available. Connecting an additional sign-in method requires fresh confirmation of your existing Rummit account and separate provider authorization; an email match does not automatically merge accounts or combine order history. Rummit logout clears the local Google SDK session but does not revoke Google’s remote authorization; manage that separately in your Google account settings.

Rummit provides listing, discovery, bidding, ordering and communication tools for actual secondhand clothing. The identified seller supplies the item. Commercial resellers must not describe themselves as private household sellers to avoid applicable obligations. Seller information review is separate from email verification and item moderation.

Listing and seller responsibilities

Offer only items you own or are authorized to sell, with photos and content you may lawfully use. Describe brand, size, fabric, measurements, condition, repairs and material flaws accurately; disclose uncertainty. Do not conceal defects with cutouts or edits. Real-photo spins must show the same actual item.

Illegal, stolen or counterfeit goods, weapons, explicit sexual content, threats, harassment, impersonation and unauthorized personal information are prohibited. New/changed listing photos need human review. Review does not authenticate a brand, guarantee an item or publish it automatically.

When assisted seller onboarding is available, provide accurate legal/business/contact information and participate in the operator's private review. Never upload or email government document numbers or scans through ordinary support. An approval can expire, be suspended or require a new review after changes. It is not a payment, authenticity or conduct guarantee.

You retain your content ownership. You authorize the processing, storage and display needed to provide the service you request, including selected public listings and necessary moderation, support and recovery. This permission does not authorize unrelated advertising or sale of your private captures; disposal follows the privacy notice.

Claims, bids and the sale

Read the actual item, condition, price, delivery charge, arrangement and deadline before acting. Contact the seller through in-app messaging to resolve missing details. A waitlist does not reserve an item.

A successful fixed-price claim creates a time-limited reservation. A valid winning bid can create an order reservation after the displayed auction closes. Bid only if you intend and can afford to complete the purchase if selected. Minimum next bids and closing times shown by the app govern submission; a bid is not a transfer of funds.

For this manual-payment flow, the seller's payment-confirmed acknowledgment accepts the order on the disclosed listing and agreed delivery terms. Earlier reservation and bid states alone do not mean payment is verified or that funds are held. This rule does not override a binding agreement the participants separately make or rights and obligations imposed by applicable law.

Unpaid reservations may be cancelled or expire at their displayed deadline. If money was already sent, report it through the order and contact the seller before the deadline. The reported-transfer state requires seller review; a cancellation/expiry in the app never reverses a transfer. Do not deliberately confirm a payment you have not checked.

Direct payments, prices and fees

Amounts are in Philippine pesos. The current release charges no platform commission or listing fee. The listing price, agreed shipping and any charge by your chosen external payment provider still apply. A future platform charge must be disclosed and accepted before the affected action.

Pay directly to the seller through the arrangement shown and agreed for the order. Confirm recipient, amount and reference before using a bank or wallet. Never share financial-service passwords, codes or full card credentials.

Payment sent is the buyer's report; Seller confirmed is the seller's manual acknowledgment. Rummit has no connected payment capture, independent provider verification, escrow, automatic refund or buyer-protection payout. A screenshot, review or badge does not prove a transfer is valid. No actual money should be sent through a preview-only checkout.

Delivery, cancellation and problems

Agree shipping/meetup, amount, delivery details and any item-specific return arrangement before paying. Sellers must provide truthful dispatch information and carry out their agreement. Seller-entered packed/dispatched/tracking updates are not independent carrier scans. Buyers should promptly report a missing or materially misdescribed item and preserve relevant evidence.

Before payment, cancel an unpaid hold through the app where available. After payment, request cancellation, return or refund from the seller and open an order-related support request if needed. For non-delivery, material misdescription, counterfeit or other unlawful goods, follow applicable mandatory rights; a seller's policy cannot remove them. There is no blanket all-sales-final rule.

Use You → Support requests → Order problem with the order ID, issue and requested outcome. Keep evidence relevant and remove unrelated private details. The operator reviews platform records, contacts participants as needed and records the outcome. We cannot reverse an external transfer or promise another party will refund it. An unresolved internal complaint does not prevent use of your bank/wallet remedies, DTI, courts or other competent authority. Applicable statutory escalation rights, including the Internet Transactions Act's internal-redress rules, remain available.

When this control is enabled, after a human review of the order, relevant evidence and participant contact attempts, the operator may mark the platform workflow Closed by support. The label and recorded participant-visible summary do not confirm buyer receipt, independently verify an external payment or refund, release a sold item for sale, or remove either party’s legal obligations. The original fulfillment and manual-payment records remain as recorded. Contact support with the order reference to request reconsideration, or email support@rummit.app if you cannot sign in. This administrative label does not restrict the applicable external remedies described above.

Safety, reports and appeals

Questions, seller answers and added seller notes marked public are visible on the lot. Keep them relevant and avoid contact details, personal information, payment credentials or identity documents. Authors can withdraw their questions; reports and abuse evidence may remain restricted to the operator for the purposes described in the privacy notice. An added seller note does not silently rewrite the original lot description or release an existing bid or order obligation.

Use the listing/shop/message menu to report specific content, or block an abusive account. Blocking does not erase an existing order or resolve money already sent. The operator can reject a listing, hide content or restrict an account with a recorded reason. A confirmed email is not verified identity.

Request reconsideration through You → Support requests → Appeal a decision, or email support@rummit.app if you cannot sign in. Identify the affected decision and relevant facts. Reinstatement needs a separate decision; other restrictions can remain. An appeal does not rewrite order or payment history.

Godwin Laureto performs initial daily listing, report, dispute and support review. Automated acknowledgments and alerts are labelled and do not replace review. No instant-response or guaranteed resolution deadline is promised. If coverage or capacity is inadequate, new trading may be paused while existing obligations can still be resolved.

Privacy, closure and availability

The privacy notice explains local captures, selected marketplace uploads, seller information, service providers, rights and retention. Deleting your online account does not erase the separate local collection. Immediate deletion requires account confirmation and checks unresolved obligations. When an obligation blocks that path, the available in-app deletion review lets you initiate a separate privacy decision without emailing or calling. If normal sign-in is unavailable, the available Account privacy flow proves control of the registered account email with an action-bound six-digit code and can only request, read or cancel that deletion review. It creates no account or normal sign-in session and never lifts a restriction. New commitments involving the account pause while the request is pending; existing resolution, export and support remain available while account access remains. You can cancel before completion through fresh account confirmation.

A human may remove the account and public presence while preserving specifically justified transaction or case evidence under recorded necessity review. The initial request review commitment is seven days; selected retained evidence receives a next review within 31 days. Those are Rummit review procedures, not statutory retention lengths, guaranteed order-resolution dates or automatic destruction deadlines. Historical safety/order relationships and recovery copies are not all immediately erased. Account deletion never verifies delivery, confirms an external payment/refund, releases sold stock, cancels another party’s lawful claim or lifts a ban. The private receipt reports status only and grants no account, transaction or evidence access.

The service has finite limits and can be interrupted by maintenance, connectivity or providers. After an uncertain response, check the current order before retrying; a timeout is not proof an action failed. The free initial infrastructure has not been proven for 1,000 simultaneous users. No uptime, successful sale or authenticity guarantee is made.

AI garment generation, escrow and automated refunds are not launch features. Optional outbid, watched-lot-ending and matching-find alerts may be available when the service is configured, you choose the relevant alerts and iOS authorizes an acknowledged device registration. Alerts are best effort: they may be delayed, duplicated or missing, and an APNs receipt does not prove device delivery. Always check the current item, bid, order and deadline in Rummit; a notification never reserves an item, confirms a payment, pauses an auction or extends a deadline. You can save fit/interests with alerts off and change notification permission in iOS Settings. Preview-only screens do not enable unavailable services.

Applicable rights and changes

Philippine law applies subject to any mandatory protections and competent authority's rules. These terms do not waive non-excludable privacy or consumer rights, excuse unlawful conduct, impose mandatory arbitration or invent a liability cap.

Material changes to trading rules or charges will be shown before the affected new action and require the current version where appropriate. A published version is preserved with its receipt; it is not silently replaced. Restrictions and termination do not cancel an existing legal payment, delivery or dispute obligation.

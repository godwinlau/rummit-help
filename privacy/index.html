Who is responsible

Rummit is operated by Godwin Laureto, Laureto's Hilltop House, Buda, Marilog District, Davao City, Davao del Sur, Philippines. Godwin Laureto is the initial privacy and support contact at support@rummit.app.

This notice covers the iOS app, marketplace and help site. The initial marketplace is for people aged 18 or older. Contact us if you believe a minor has supplied account information.

Your private finds stay on your device

Your private clothing captures and collection are stored on your device. We do not automatically upload your photo library or whole collection. Publishing or preparing an online listing uploads only the selected photos and listing details used by that flow. Signing out or deleting the marketplace account does not erase the separate local collection or copies you export.

Cutouts and label reading use Apple Vision on your device. Real-photo spins display the actual captured frames. AI garment generation is disabled; no garment photos are sent to an AI 3D provider for this release.

Information and who can see it

Account
Email, display name, password hash where applicable, account identifiers, sessions, email verification/recovery challenges and policy receipts. If you choose Sign in with Apple or Google, this also includes the provider identity relationship, the name and email the provider shares, and short-lived authentication and account-confirmation proof records. Apple may supply a private relay email. The backend represents the stable provider identifier with a private keyed hash. Apple credentials needed to manage and revoke its sign-in are encrypted on the backend; Rummit does not retain Google access or refresh tokens on its backend. Passwords are not stored as readable passwords.
Your fit and interests
The top sizes, waist choices, followed clothing categories and alert choices you explicitly save, with completion/version records. These are private account preferences, not public body measurements or an advertising profile. Matching uses supported seller-entered tag sizes and explicitly labeled garment measurements; it does not infer your body from photos or guarantee that an item will fit. Compare the item’s actual measurements and condition before buying. The example selections in a design or preview are not your saved choices.
Saved items and searches
Signed-in marketplace watchlists are private account records used to show your saved lots and, if enabled, identify watched items nearing their deadline. Guest bookmarks and local search history can remain on the device rather than becoming an online watchlist. Local saved searches do not by themselves create a server subscription or notification guarantee. They are separate from the public questions, listings or shop follows you explicitly create.
Notification registration
When optional push delivery is available and you choose alerts, iOS asks for notification permission. An acknowledged registration associates the device’s APNs token, installation/registration identifiers, authorization status and selected delivery scope with your current account and session. The token is encrypted on Rummit’s backend. Small delivery records contain event type, opaque item/drop references, attempt/status and expiry information. They do not contain private chat text, payment instructions, contact details or garment photos.
Listings and shops
Selected photos, measurements, descriptions, condition, flaws, prices, shop profile and review records. Published information is visible to other people and can be copied by them. Storefront settings can show selected public lots, shelves and a sold archive. Choosing a shop logo explicitly uploads a separate square, greyscale copy for public display; it does not make the rest of your private collection public.
Public questions and seller updates
Questions you choose to post, seller answers and added seller notes appear publicly on the lot. Question authors’ account names and email addresses are not displayed in that history; the backend keeps the author relationship for withdrawal, safety and request limits. Seller answers show the shop handle. Do not post contact details or unrelated personal information. Withdrawing a question removes its public text and answer. A private report can retain the text as safety evidence. You can find and withdraw your own questions from Account tools, reached through Settings → Help and buyer protection → Account and safety, including when the lot is no longer accessible to you.
Orders and conversations
Bids, waitlists, reservations, orders, direct seller payment instructions, manual payment updates, delivery/contact details and participant messages. Order participants receive the information needed to carry out their order. In-app messaging lets a buyer contact a seller before ordering without publicly posting private contact information.
Seller review
When assisted seller onboarding is enabled, legal name, individual/business category, trading activity, business/contact address, mobile, confirmed account email, notice acceptance and review status. The operator can record the kind of identity or registration document presented, a case reference, decision, reason and review expiry. The app does not request or store government document numbers, scans or photographs. Do not attach these to support messages. A changed profile needs review again.
Safety and operation
Reports, blocks, support tickets/replies, operator decisions, request limits, operation/retry records, media metadata and encrypted recovery copies. Relevant operators can access private information needed for support, safety and seller review; it is not a public identity register.

Public browsing works without an account. Private conversation, order and support access is restricted to the relevant participants or authorized operator. A confirmed email or reviewed seller information is not a guarantee of authenticity, future conduct or payment.

Why information is used

We use account information to provide requested sign-in, recovery and account functions; listing and order information to provide the transactions you request; messages and support records to deliver your communications and resolve issues; and seller information to review trading eligibility and meet applicable marketplace obligations. Your saved fit, category and watchlist choices provide the matching and optional alerts you request. Alert permission is separate from accepting these terms or completing account setup: sizes and interests can be saved with all alerts off. Enabling an alert requires both the relevant choice and an authorized, acknowledged device registration.

We use proportionate security, fraud, moderation and recovery records to protect the service and its users, subject to balancing those interests against your rights. We also process information when required by applicable law. Camera/photo access and publishing/export choices are separate choices; accepting terms does not authorize unrelated processing or remove your privacy rights.

Do not enter passwords, verification codes, full payment-card credentials, identity-document numbers or unrelated private details into listings, messages or support requests. Remove private information from publicly shared photos. Marketplace uploads are decoded and re-encoded with ancillary location metadata removed.

Service providers and international processing

Apple provides Sign in with Apple when available and you choose that button. The native Apple sheet asks you to authorize sharing your name and email, with Hide My Email available through Apple. Rummit sends the resulting authentication proofs to Apple for verification and token management; it does not receive your Apple password. Rummit keeps the provider credentials needed for sign-in management and access revocation encrypted on its backend. Google provides Sign in with Google through its iOS SDK and account-consent interface when enabled and you choose it. Google shares the signed identity proof and permitted basic profile information, such as name and email, for sign-in; Rummit validates the proof against Google’s public signing keys and the requested account action. Rummit does not receive your Google password or request access to Google mail, contacts, Drive or other Google account content. The native Google SDK may keep its own sign-in credentials/cache on the device. Rummit sign-out or account deletion clears that local SDK session; this is not revocation of Google’s remote app authorization. You can manage that authorization in your Google account settings. Authentication does not send garment photos, listings, private messages or payment details to either sign-in provider. Each provider’s own account and privacy terms also apply.

Using a provider with an email already belonging to a Rummit account does not silently merge accounts or move purchase history. Connecting another sign-in method requires a fresh proof of the current Rummit account and separate authorization of the provider being added. Proofs are tied to that account/session and expire; an old proof or email match is not permission to link another account.

Apple Push Notification service (APNs) receives the device token and the allow-listed notification payload when an eligible alert is submitted. Lock-screen messages are generic, such as “You’ve been outbid” or “A watched lot is ending,” and prompt you to open Rummit. They do not include bid amounts, listing titles, private messages, delivery addresses or payment details. Opaque event/item/drop references and a registration scope support navigation; the app checks the current signed-in account and current item access before opening a destination. A generic message can still reveal that Rummit activity occurred to someone who can see your screen. Manage notification display and permission in iOS Settings. APNs acceptance is a provider receipt, not proof that a message reached or was displayed on the device. Delivery is best effort, may be late, duplicated or absent, and does not pause an auction or extend a deadline.

Supabase hosts the database, private media and backend functions; the current project is in Singapore. Resend delivers account verification and recovery emails through the configured Tokyo sending region. A code is sent only to the account email; operational emails use fixed condition codes rather than customer message content.

GitHub hosts this static help site, runs private operational checks and stores encrypted recovery copies as private artifacts. The backup worker has no decryption key or database password. Namecheap forwards support email to the owner's Google Gmail inbox. Emailing support shares your sender details and message with those providers and the owner.

Cloudflare Turnstile provides an account-security check when enabled. The app opens Rummit’s restricted security page in an embedded browser, which loads Cloudflare’s verification service. Cloudflare receives the network address and browser/device signals associated with that check and issues a short-lived token. Rummit validates that token on the backend for the requested account action. No password, email code, garment photo, listing text or private message is sent by Rummit to Turnstile as part of that check. The backend verification secret stays on the backend. Cloudflare’s own provider processing and privacy terms also apply.

These providers and their subprocessors can process data outside the Philippines. We do not promise Philippine-only storage or that every provider log is held in one selected region. Provider security, retention and transfer arrangements also apply. We remain responsible for choosing and managing our service arrangements and handling your requests.

The current app has no advertising or third-party analytics SDK and does not track you across other companies' apps or sites. The Google sign-in SDK is used for the authentication function described above, not for an advertising or analytics feature in Rummit. Ordinary static help and policy pages use no application cookies, analytics, external fonts or scripts. The separate security-check page loads the Cloudflare scripts and embedded verification frame needed for Turnstile when enabled; it is not an ordinary help page. Hosting, email and network providers can still keep operational access and delivery records.

Retention, disposal and recovery

We retain information for the purpose for which it is needed, including an active account, open order, unresolved support/safety case or an applicable legal obligation. The operator reviews retained records monthly; a record without a continuing purpose must be removed or redacted through a recorded disposal action. This is a manual review procedure, not an automatic age-based deletion promise.

Account and seller profile
While the account or seller relationship is active. Account closure removes or redacts direct account/profile information as described below. Seller approval evidence is not carried forward as approval after a disaster restore.
Fit, interests and saved items
Private size, waist, category and alert preferences remain until you change them or delete the account, subject to a specific continuing security/legal purpose described in this notice. Account watchlist entries remain until you remove them or the account is deleted. Guest bookmarks and local search history remain separate device data until removed or the relevant app data is erased. Signing out does not itself erase the private garment collection.
Orders, messages and safety cases
While needed to complete the transaction, resolve a dispute or safety concern, prevent a specific continuing abuse, or satisfy an applicable obligation. Each monthly review records the continuing purpose or disposal action. A documented legal/safety hold must identify its reason, owner and next review date; a hold is not permission for indefinite unrelated retention.
Support email
The same purpose review applies to forwarded correspondence and attachments in the owner's support inbox. Deleting an in-app ticket does not automatically delete an email copy.
Operational expiry
Sessions expire after 30 days. Email codes expire after 15 minutes; email verification and recovery challenge records become eligible for bounded cleanup 24 hours after expiry. Apple and Google authorization challenges, and current-account linking/reauthentication proofs, expire after five minutes. Expired provider challenges become eligible for bounded cleanup. Current-account proof attempts, including rejected attempts used for rate limits, are retained for a one-hour attempt window and then become eligible for bounded cleanup. Retry receipts expire after 24 hours. Eligibility is not an exact physical deletion time.
Push work and revocation markers
Each push delivery job expires no later than 24 hours after creation. Expired jobs, or finished jobs older than a day, become eligible for cleanup in bounded batches; failures or pauses can delay physical removal. When the backend processes sign-out, account deletion or device revocation, it clears the active account/session association and encrypted device token and cancels pending delivery work. An offline or unconfirmed revocation may need retry before that server cleanup is acknowledged. Small unowned installation/generation markers and one-way authentication hashes can remain to prevent a delayed registration request from re-enabling a revoked device. Their store has finite capacity, with a separate cap on new anonymous revocation markers. These security tombstones are included in the operator’s monthly purpose review; no automatic age-based tombstone deletion is promised. A provider request already accepted before revocation may still arrive.
Recovery copies
Application snapshot pruning protects the newest two verified snapshots and removes older copies in bounded batches. This is a count policy; cleanup failures can retain older copies longer. Independent GitHub encrypted artifacts specify seven days after upload. That does not control provider logs or separately downloaded operator copies, which are restricted and included in the operator's purpose review.

A recovery copy may predate a deletion. Restored databases remain inactive, credentials are invalidated, and post-snapshot deletion, moderation and order changes require reconciliation before exposure. Restored seller profiles require fresh review. Restored Google identity mappings are paused for explicit recovery reconciliation; restored provider proofs are invalidated. Restored push registrations lose their account/session association and encrypted tokens, and queued/running push jobs are cancelled. A restore does not automatically resume old sign-in proofs or delivery work. If authoritative changes cannot be established, the recovery guard blocks activation; it cannot recreate unknown changes or promise no data loss.

Your rights and account deletion

From You, you can manage blocks and support requests, export account information, or request account deletion. The export is a bounded, paginated view of customer-facing sections; it is not every internal record or a frozen database snapshot. Report a specific listing, shop, message or public question from its menu. Public-question reports and their evidence are available to the authorized safety operator, not to other buyers or sellers.

Deletion requires password confirmation or recent authorization with a connected Apple or Google method, and checks unresolved paid/shipped orders, reported transfers and active winning bid or seller-auction obligations. Successful deletion disables the account and revokes sessions, replaces public identity, redacts sent message bodies and support content, redacts public questions, answers and associated question-report snapshots involving the deleted account or its shop, clears public seller notes and shop-logo selections, removes seller review profile data, saved account watchlists and private fit/interest preferences, revokes active notification registrations and cancels pending delivery work, and clears completed/cancelled buyer delivery contact details. Historical order, bid and safety/audit relationships are not all erased; opaque references are not necessarily anonymous. Uploaded media is queued for physical deletion with retries. Local captures, user exports and previously emailed copies are separate.

For access, correction, erasure/blocking, objection, portability where applicable, or a privacy complaint, email support@rummit.app. Identify the relevant account or record and the requested outcome. We may verify ownership without asking for a password or one-time code, protect other people's data, and explain an applicable reason if a request cannot be fully fulfilled. You may also raise concerns with the Philippine National Privacy Commission; contacting us does not waive that right.

Security and updates

We use hashed passwords, revocable credentials, device Keychain storage for sessions, private uploaded originals, access controls and encrypted recovery copies. These safeguards reduce risk; no system can promise absolute security or uninterrupted availability. Finite service limits can temporarily reject requests.

Godwin Laureto performs initial daily support and safety review. If coverage is unavailable or safety/capacity cannot be maintained, new trading can be paused while existing order-resolution access remains available. Material changes to processing will be explained before the affected new processing. Published policy versions and their acceptance receipts are kept separately; a later version does not silently overwrite a version you accepted.
